Prepare better. Speak clearer. Show up with confidence.
The Interview Prep Sprint is a focused 
2
-session course for marketing professionals who have an upcoming interview and want to prepare properly.
It is designed for people applying for roles in marketing, brand, growth, digital marketing, communications or related fields.
This is not a generic interview coaching session.

Who This Course Is For
This course is for:
Marketing professionals with an upcoming interview
Brand professionals preparing for a new role
Growth marketers interviewing for growth or acquisition roles
Digital marketers preparing for digital, content or performance roles
Marketing executives moving into bigger roles
Marketing managers preparing for leadership interviews
Career switchers interviewing for marketing roles
What You Will Learn
By the end of this course, you will understand:
How to prepare properly for a marketing interview
How to study the company, role and market before the interview
How to connect your experience to what the employer needs
How to answer common marketing interview questions
How to speak about campaigns, results and business impact
How to explain your thinking without sounding rehearsed
How to handle questions around strategy, execution, data and stakeholders
How to ask strong questions at the end of the interview
Course Structure
Session 1: Interview Strategy &amp; Positioning
Duration: 60 minutes
This session helps you understand the role, the company and the story you need to tell.
We will review your background, the role you are interviewing for and the key areas you need to prepare.
This session covers:
Understanding the job description
Identifying what the interviewer is likely looking for
Clarifying your strongest marketing stories
Positioning your experience around impact
Preparing answers for likely questions
Connecting your work to business outcomes
Session 2: Mock Interview &amp; Feedback
Duration: 60 minutes
This session gives you the opportunity to practise before the real interview.
You will go through a guided mock interview and receive practical feedback on your answers, confidence, clarity and delivery.
This session covers:
Mock interview practice
Feedback on your answers
Improving weak or unclear responses
Strengthening your examples
Preparing for difficult marketing questions
Improving your confidence and delivery
Questions to ask the interviewer
Final preparation before the interview

A clearer understanding of the role

A better way to explain your marketing experience
Improved confidence before the interview

Switching Into Marketing
A practical course for people who want to start a career in marketing, digital or brand management.
Breaking into marketing can feel confusing when you are coming from another background.
You may know you are interested in marketing, but you are not sure where to start, what skills matter, how to position your previous experience or what kind of roles to apply for.
This course is designed to help you make that transition with more clarity.
It will help you understand how marketing works, the different career paths available, the skills you need to build and how to present yourself as someone who can bring value, even without a traditional marketing background.
This is not a theory-heavy course.
It is a practical guide for people who want to move from interest to direction.
Who This Course Is For
This course is for:
Career switchers moving into marketing
Professionals interested in digital marketing
People who want to move into brand management
Graduates exploring marketing as a career path
Creatives who want to understand marketing better
Business professionals trying to transition into growth, brand or communications roles
Anyone unsure how to start or position themselves for marketing opportunities
What You Will Learn
By the end of this course, you will understand:
What marketing really is beyond social media and campaigns
The difference between marketing, digital marketing, brand management, growth and communications
The key skills employers look for in entry-level and transition roles
How to identify the right marketing path for your personality, strengths and interests
How to position your previous experience as relevant marketing value
How to build a simple portfolio even if you have not worked in marketing before
How to improve your CV and LinkedIn for marketing roles
How to prepare for marketing interviews
How to avoid common mistakes career switchers make
Course Modules
Module 1: Understanding Marketing, Digital and Brand Management
Get clear on what marketing really is, the difference between marketing, digital marketing, brand management, growth and communications, and where each path can lead.
Module 2: Finding Your Best Fit
Understand your strengths, interests and transferable skills, then identify the marketing path that makes the most sense for you.
Module 3: Building Your Foundation
Learn the core skills, tools and knowledge you need to start positioning yourself for marketing, digital or brand roles.
Module 4: Creating Proof Without Experience
Learn how to build sample work, case studies, personal projects and a simple portfolio that shows your thinking, even if you have not worked in marketing before.
Module 5: Positioning Yourself for Opportunities
Learn how to improve your CV, LinkedIn profile and interview answers so you can present yourself as a credible candidate for marketing roles.
What You Will Leave With
A clearer understanding of marketing career paths
A stronger sense of where you fit
A practical transition plan
Better CV and LinkedIn positioning
Ideas for building a beginner portfolio
More confidence applying for marketing, digital and brand roles
A better understanding of how to speak about your value
